Official Hackathon Rules

👥 Team Composition

  • Each team must comprise exactly 5 students.
  • Each team must consist of at least 1 girl (Teams with 5 girls are not allowed; at least 1 boy is compulsory).
  • Students must be currently enrolled in a recognized engineering college in India.
  • Mixed teams from different colleges are not permitted (all members must be from the same institution, though branches can differ).
  • One team leader must be designated for all communication.

🎯 Selection & Participation

  • B.Tech and B.E. students from any engineering college (including WCE) are eligible. PhD scholars and non-students are not eligible.
  • Each team must select exactly one problem statement.
  • Once confirmed (after payment), the task assignment cannot be changed.
  • Teams must work only on their assigned task for the duration of the event.

💳 Registration & Refund Policy

  • Registration requires a valid UTR/transaction ID from UPI payment. Confirmation is issued post-verification.
  • Fraudulent entries will result in immediate disqualification.
  • No refunds will be provided once confirmed, even for voluntary withdrawal or failure to attend.
  • If the event is rescheduled, fees remain non-refundable.

⚖️ Evaluation & Jury

  • All team members must be present for the jury evaluation on Friday.
  • Jury evaluation is based on: experimental accuracy, methodology, report quality, and viva responses.
  • The jury's decision is final and binding. No appeals will be entertained.
  • Results and scores will be announced at the Friday prize distribution.
